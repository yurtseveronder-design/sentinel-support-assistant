# Northwind Club subscriptions

Doc ID: POL-SUB-01

## Plans

Northwind Club has an annual plan at £120 per year, billed upfront, and a monthly plan at £12 per month.

## Cancelling and renewal

A cancellation counts only if the customer received a confirmation email at least 48 hours before the renewal date. If the customer has that confirmation email and was charged anyway, refund the full renewal charge, because the mistake is ours. If the cancellation came less than 48 hours before renewal, the charge stands. For a late cancellation an agent may give one courtesy refund per customer, within 14 days of the charge, and must tag the ticket "courtesy".

## Changing plan mid-cycle

An annual plan downgrade mid-cycle takes effect at the next renewal. No pro-rata refund is given for a mid-cycle downgrade, unless the downgrade is requested within 30 days of purchase or renewal. In that case the unused full months are refunded pro-rata.
