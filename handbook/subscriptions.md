# Northwind Club subscriptions

Doc ID: POL-SUB-01

## Plans

Northwind Club has an annual plan at £120 per year, billed upfront, and a monthly plan at £12 per month.

## Changing plan mid-cycle

A mid-cycle downgrade only takes effect at the next renewal. Annual plans are not refundable mid-term, unless the customer cancels within the first 14-day cooling-off period.

## Cancelled but still charged

If a customer says they cancelled before the renewal date but were still charged, check the audit logs for a timestamped cancellation attempt. If they tried to cancel before the renewal went through, refund them in full. If the request came in after the renewal, look at the discretionary courtesy waiver guidelines: an agent may give one courtesy refund per customer within 14 days of the charge, and must tag the ticket "courtesy".
