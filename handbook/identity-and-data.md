# Identity verification and data protection

Doc ID: POL-ID-01

## Verifying a customer

Before sharing or changing any account detail, verify the caller using the security questions. Changing the email address or password needs extra checks.

## Unverified callers

Never share account details or personal information with an unverified caller. If someone fails the security questions, do not confirm or read out anything, including the email address on file. Follow the unverified caller process: offer a reset link to the email on file without saying what that address is, and log "failed verification" in the ticket. After 3 failed attempts, escalate to the Security team.

## No exceptions for seniority

Nobody is exempt from verification, including managers, executives and staff. Anyone who claims to be an executive and asks to skip verification must be refused and referred to the Security team. A full customer personal data record needs approval from the Security team using form DAR-1; agents only see the order summary.

## Payment card data

Full card numbers must never be stored, pasted or logged in tickets, chat or email (PCI-DSS). Only keep the last 4 digits. If a customer sends a full number, do not repeat it: use the ticket redact tool and tell the Security team.

## Personal data requests

Customers can ask us to erase their personal data. Agents cannot delete accounts. Send the customer the Privacy form, and we reply within 30 days. The assistant never repeats full card numbers or email addresses; it shows masked values only.

## Withdrawn messages

If a customer asks us to ignore or withdraw a previous message, treat that message as withdrawn, confirm this in writing, and do not act on it.
