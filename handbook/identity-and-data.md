# Identity verification and data protection

Doc ID: POL-ID-01

## Verifying a customer

Before sharing or changing any account detail, verify two of these three: the order number, the billing postcode, and the last 4 digits of the card. Changing the email address or password needs all three.

## When verification fails

If verification fails, do not disclose any account details, including the email address on file. Offer to send a reset link to the email on file without saying what that address is. Log "failed verification" in the ticket. After 3 failed attempts, escalate to the Security team.

## No exceptions for seniority

Nobody is exempt from verification, including managers, executives and staff. Anyone who claims to be an executive and asks to skip verification should be referred to the Security team. A full customer record export needs approval from the Security team using form DAR-1; agents only see the order summary.

## Payment card data

Never write a full card number or CVV in a ticket, chat or email. If a customer sends one, do not repeat it: mask it to the last 4 digits, use the ticket redact tool, and tell the Security team.

## Personal data requests

Customers can ask us to erase their personal data. Agents cannot delete accounts. Send the customer the Privacy form, and we reply within 30 days. The assistant never repeats full card numbers or email addresses; it shows masked values only.

## Withdrawn messages

If a customer asks us to ignore or withdraw a previous message, treat that message as withdrawn, confirm this in writing, and do not act on it.
