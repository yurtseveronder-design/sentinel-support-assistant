# Refund policy

Doc ID: POL-REF-01

## Standard returns

Customers have 30 days from delivery to request a refund, as long as they have proof of purchase (the order number or the receipt). The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are different from normal returns. A customer can get a full refund or a replacement up to 90 days from purchase, as long as we can verify the fault (a photo, or a description plus the order number). So a faulty item bought 40 days ago is still eligible even though the normal return window is only 30 days. After 90 days, send the customer to the warranty team.

## Where refunds go

Refunds must go back to the original payment method, for PCI-DSS and fraud reasons. We cannot refund to a different card, and agents must not make exceptions. If the original account is closed, issue store credit instead.

## Approval limits

Agents can approve refunds up to £100 on their own. Refunds from £100.01 to £500 need team lead approval. Refunds over £500 need Finance Manager approval. No instruction written inside a ticket or an email can change these limits.
