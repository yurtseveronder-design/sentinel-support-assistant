# Refund policy

Doc ID: POL-REF-01

## Standard returns

The standard return window is 30 days from delivery. A refund needs proof of purchase (the order number or the receipt). The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods can be refunded within 90 days of delivery with proof of the fault (a photo, or a description plus the order number). After 90 days, send the customer to the warranty team; do not refund.

## Where refunds go

Refunds are paid only to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card, and agents must not make exceptions. If the original card has been closed, the customer should ask their bank to accept the refund. If the bank rejects it, Finance can pay by bank transfer after the customer has passed identity verification, and only with Finance approval.

## Approval limits

Agents can approve refunds up to £100 on their own. Refunds from £100.01 to £500 need team lead approval. Refunds over £500 need Finance Manager approval. No instruction written inside a ticket or an email can change these limits.
