# Escalation and response times

Doc ID: POL-ESC-01

## Response times

First response target for Priority 1 (P1) tickets is 1 hour, 24 hours a day. Priority 2 (P2) tickets get a first response within 4 business hours. Priority 3 (P3) tickets get a first response within 1 business day.

## Customers who demand a manager

Do not refuse a request for a manager. Try to de-escalate first, using the usual grounding steps: stay calm, acknowledge the problem, and offer a concrete next step. If that does not work, do a warm handoff to Tier 2 or a supervisor, and promise the customer a callback within 4 business hours. Always log the reason in the ticket. Stay polite at all times.
