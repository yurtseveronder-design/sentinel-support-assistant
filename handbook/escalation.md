# Escalation and response times

Doc ID: POL-ESC-01

## Response times

First response target for Priority 1 (P1) tickets is 1 hour, 24 hours a day. Priority 2 (P2) tickets get a first response within 4 business hours. Priority 3 (P3) tickets get a first response within 1 business day.

## Customers who demand a manager

Do not refuse a request for a manager. First try to resolve the problem. Then offer a callback from a team lead within 4 working hours. Transfer the customer immediately (a warm handoff) only if they threaten legal action, report a safety concern, ask for a refund over £500, or have asked for a manager twice. Always log the reason in the ticket. The supervisor on duty is reached in the #support-supervisor channel. Stay polite at all times.
