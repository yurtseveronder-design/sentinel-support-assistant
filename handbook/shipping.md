# Shipping policy

Doc ID: POL-SHP-01

## UK delivery options

UK Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. UK Express delivery arrives the next working day. Express orders must be placed before 2pm.

## Delivery addresses

We do not ship to UK PO boxes or parcel lockers. We need a physical street address, and the recipient has to sign for the package.

## Damaged in transit

If an item arrives damaged, start a damaged-in-transit claim. The customer must send photos of the item and the packaging within 7 days of delivery. Once we confirm the damage, send a replacement by express shipping at no extra cost, if the replacement is in stock. If the original order was shipped express, the express fee stays covered by us. If the replacement is out of stock, offer a refund or a wait for new stock.
