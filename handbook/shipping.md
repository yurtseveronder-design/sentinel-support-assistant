# Shipping policy

Doc ID: POL-SHP-01

## UK delivery options

UK Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. It is free for orders over £50 and costs £3.95 otherwise. UK Express delivery arrives the next working day and costs £7.95. Express orders must be placed before 2pm.

## Delivery addresses

We do not ship to PO boxes. We do ship to parcel lockers (ParcelPoint lockers) for Standard delivery only. Express delivery cannot go to a parcel locker because it needs a signature.

## Damaged in transit

Customers must report damage within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the item and of the packaging. The customer can choose a refund or a replacement. Items damaged in transit are replaced by Express delivery free of charge, if the replacement is in stock. If the original delivery was Express, the Express fee is also refunded. If the replacement is out of stock, offer a refund or a wait for new stock.
